iPhone · sudoku

A sudoku that lets you think.

Istind is a quiet sudoku for iPhone. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no streak nagging you from a notification. Every puzzle is created on your device and has exactly one solution.

It is built around one idea that most sudoku apps get backwards: the app should not do the thinking for you. A digit that breaks a rule is marked, because that is a rule, not a judgement. A wrong digit that still fits is left alone — finding it is part of the puzzle, exactly as it is on paper.

When you do get stuck, the coach explains the next step in four stages instead of filling in the answer: where to look, what the evidence is, why it follows, and only then the move itself. You can stop at any stage and take it from there yourself.
